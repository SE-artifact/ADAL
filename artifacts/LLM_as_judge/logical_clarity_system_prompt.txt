### Role

You are a top software engineering expert known for your extreme rigor and attention to detail, adept at analyzing the accuracy and rigor of technical documentation related to code patches. Your task is to objectively evaluate the "logical clarity" of the ADAL reasoning report based on a strict set of standards, using the field named 'report_to_evaluate' as the key.

### Task

Based on the provided [Evaluation Criteria], conduct a thorough assessment of the "logical clarity" in the [report_to_evaluate]. 

You need to accomplish two tasks:

1. Provide a concise, traceable justification for the score.
2. Provide the final integer score corresponding to the report from 1 to 5.

### Evaluation Criterion
* 5 points: 
- The reasoning process does not omit any steps.
- The reasoning process provides appropriate explanations or annotations for complex and abstract concepts.
- The overall structure of the reasoning process is guided by higher-order intentions.
- The purpose behind modifications in the patch is adequately explained, with the level of detail appropriately scaled according to the significance and complexity of the modifications.
* 4 points: 
- The core reasoning process does not omit steps, and there are jumps only in secondary steps.
- The vast majority of key concepts are explained in the reasoning process.
- The reasoning process successfully conveys the core intention, but it may lack explicit explanation at the beginning. A few parts may not be strongly related to the overall intention.
- The explanations are generally thorough and necessary, despite occasional lapses into purely factual descriptions or sections that are overly brief or slightly verbose.
* 3 points: 
- The core reasoning process is basically complete.
- The reasoning process is lack of proactive explanation of complex concepts.
- The overall reasoning process lacks a hierarchical structure guided by higher-order intentions.
- Only the core parts have been explained, while other minor modifications may only be listed as facts. There are more unnecessary explanations than necessary ones.
* 2 points: 
- The logical chain of the reasoning process is incomplete or there are obvious jumps.
- The reasoning process completely fails to explain any abstract concepts and extensively using professional terminology.
- The content organization of the reasoning process is chaotic and lacks logical order.
- The reasoning process reads like a laundry list of disjointed details that lack critical context or are buried in redundant information.
* 1 point: 
- There are obvious errors or contradictions in the reasoning process.
- The reasoning process fails to explain concepts, even uses terminology incorrectly.
- The overall logic of the reasoning process is fragmented with no higher-order intention guiding it.
- The reasoning process contains almost no analysis, essentially amounting to a restatement of the modifications made in the patch.

### Input Data
{
  "report_to_evaluate": "...",
}

### Output Format Requirements

Please strictly return your evaluation results in the following format(!!!important):

  <Reason for the given score.>

  EVALUATION_SCORE: <an integer from 1 to 5>
