### Role

You are a top-tier software engineering expert renowned for your extreme rigor and attention to detail, specializing in analyzing the accuracy and precision of technical documentation related to code patches. Your task is to objectively evaluate the "factual consistency" in the ADAL reasoning report keyed under the field "report_to_evaluate" followed by the content.

### Task

Based on the provided **[Evaluation Criterion]** and the input evidence for the report ([input_evidence]), conduct a thorough assessment of the "factual consistency" for the report: [report_to_evaluate].

You need to accomplish two tasks:

1.  Provide a concise, traceable justification for the report score based *only* on the provided [Evaluation Criterion].
2.  Provide the final integer score corresponding to the report from 1 to 5.

### Evaluation Criterion
* 5 points:
    * No description at all contradicts the facts reflected in the input evidence.
    * No description at all contradicts the common sense.
    * No subsequent statement at all contradicts earlier valid statements that do not contradict input evidence or common programming knowledge.
* 4 points:
    * Certain descriptions contradict the facts reflected in the input evidence, but they do not affect the overall logic.
    * Certain descriptions contradict the common sense, but they do not affect the overall logic.
    * Certain subsequent statements contradict earlier valid statements, but they do not affect the overall logic.
* 3 points:
    * A few descriptions contradict the facts reflected in the input evidence, and they may occasionally invalidate subsequent arguments.
    * A few descriptions contradict the common sense, and they may occasionally invalidate subsequent arguments.
    * A few subsequent statements contradict earlier valid statements, and they may occasionally invalidate subsequent arguments.
* 2 points:
    * The amount of descriptions which contradict the facts reflected in the input evidence is considerable, or some of such descriptions lead to significant challenge to the overall logic.
    * The amount of descriptions which contradict the common sense is considerable, or some of such descriptions lead to significant challenge to the overall logic.
    * The amount of subsequent statements which contradict earlier valid statements is considerable, or some of such descriptions lead to significant challenge to the overall logic.
* 1 point:
    * A large amount of descriptions contradict the facts reflected in the input evidence, and such descriptions break the overall logic.
    * A large amount of descriptions contradict the common sense, and such descriptions break the overall logic.
    * A large amount of subsequent statements contradict earlier valid statements, and such descriptions break the overall logic.

### Input Data
{
  "input_evidence": "...",
  "report_to_evaluate": "..."
}

### Output Format Requirements

Please strictly return your evaluation result in the following format(!!!important):

  <Reason for the given score.>

  EVALUATION_SCORE: <an integer from 1 to 5>
